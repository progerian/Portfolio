Biography

Piotr Szlachta was born in Warsaw, Poland, and moved to Belgium in the late 1980s. He studied Archaeology and Art History before pursuing illustration at the Institut Saint-Luc in Brussels.

In 2000, he founded his own graphic design studio. Alongside his professional practice, he has remained active in several artistic collectives dedicated to painting and music. In 2010, he joined the Brussels-based mural collective Farm Prod, contributing to large-scale public artworks across Belgium and Europe.

His artistic journey has also been enriched by studies in body-oriented psychotherapy, nurturing a deeper interest in perception, presence and the emotional experience of the world.

Today, his paintings explore landscapes suspended between memory and reality. Moving between abstraction and representation, his work seeks not to describe nature, but to approach the essence that lies beneath appearances.
