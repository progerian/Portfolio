My work begins with the landscape, though it is never truly about the landscape. Nature is a point of departure — a language through which I trace memory, emotion, silence, and the quiet structures that shape the way we perceive.

I am drawn less to what can be seen than to what remains once the anecdote fades. Forms are reduced to their essential presence; paint is left to flow, dissolve, and resurface, revealing the fragile threshold where the visible gives way to feeling.

Each painting is an attempt to draw nearer to the essence of things — not an arrival, but a path. Suspended between figuration and abstraction, presence and disappearance, I hope to open a space for contemplation, where the viewer might reconnect with a landscape of their own.
