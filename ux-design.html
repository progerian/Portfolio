UX/Design

UX & Web Conception

Design Team Leader

As Design Team Leader, I lead the UX and design work behind the ongoing redesign of the European Parliament's web ecosystem. The project is a major co-creation effort, with decisions developed collectively through a workflow involving design, business, development, integration and QA.

A key part of my role is to act as the creative link between these teams. I translate business needs, technical constraints, content requirements and user insights into concrete design solutions — framing problems, exploring directions, facilitating decisions and maintaining a coherent design vision from early UX thinking through to implementation and quality assurance.

Top Tasks & Information Architecture

The approach is built around Gerry McGovern's Top Tasks methodology. User research and task classification help identify what people actually come to the Parliament's websites to accomplish, and these priorities directly inform navigation, content hierarchy, user journeys and interface decisions.

Design System & Workflow

In parallel, we created the European Parliament Design System as a shared framework for designing and implementing the new ecosystem.

The design workflow and system have also benefited from the expertise of Vitaly Friedman, particularly around complex UX, interaction patterns and design processes.

The Top Tasks methodology and the Design System continuously inform one another: the methodology helps define what should be prioritised, while the Design System translates those principles into reusable patterns, components and interaction rules.

The result is a shared design language and working method that helps multiple teams build a more consistent, accessible and user-centred experience.

As Design Team Leader, I also contribute to numerous European Parliament side projects, including campaign, thematic and informational websites, helping ensure that they remain aligned with the broader UX principles and design direction.

Smaller projects

Alongside this large institutional work, I also take on smaller, more autonomous projects, including Atmaram and Clinique Saint-Josse.

These projects allow me to work across the full process — from information architecture and user journeys to interface design, visual identity and, where relevant, automated workflows.
