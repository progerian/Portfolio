Desertfest New York 2020

Desertfest New York is the US edition of the international Desertfest festival, dedicated to stoner rock, doom, sludge, heavy psych and underground heavy music. Launched in 2019, the New York edition brings together international heavyweights and underground acts from across the heavy music scene.

The 2020 edition, which was due to be the festival’s second edition, was cancelled due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
