The Progerians

Punk · Sludge · Doom — Brussels

The Progerians are a power trio from Brussels playing a heavy mix of stoner and sludge rock with a punk attitude, occasionally veering into grunge and hardcore territory. Formerly known as The Fabulous Progerians and The Mighty Progerians, the band released their first EP, Degeneration, in 2012.

Their sound was crystalized on the 2015 debut album The Fabulous Progerians, which led to invitations to play legendary festivals across Europe including Desertfest and Roadburn. In 2019, with the help of Mottow Soundz, they released their most ambitious record to date, Crush The Wise Men Who Refuse To Submit.
